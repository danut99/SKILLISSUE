Draw a Perfect Circle, Square or Triangle in One Stroke

Drawing a perfect circle by hand is famously hard, and Skilly makes it a daily test. Every day there's a new shape (circle, square, triangle, diamond or hexagon). Draw it in one continuous stroke and see how close to perfect you really are.

How to play

  1. Look at today's shape outline around the center dot.
  2. Draw it in one continuous stroke with your finger or mouse, and close the loop.
  3. You can draw bigger or smaller than the guide: proportions and a clean, closed outline are what count.
  4. You have 8 seconds per try and 5 tries per day. Your best drawing counts.

How scoring works

Your score (0 to 100%) rewards an accurate outline and a closed loop. The line changes color live while you draw, from cyan (great) to yellow to red. Tiny shapes, unfinished loops or drawing too close to the dot don't count.

Tips to score higher

  • Draw at a steady medium speed: too fast wobbles, too slow shakes.
  • Move your whole arm or wrist instead of just your fingers.
  • For a circle, keep the same distance from the center dot all the way around.
  • For corners (square, triangle, diamond), keep the sides straight and the corners sharp.

FAQ

Can you draw a perfect circle?

A truly perfect circle by hand is almost impossible, but scores above 95% are possible with practice. Try it with today's shape on Skilly.

Which shapes are there?

The daily shape rotates between circle, square, triangle, diamond and hexagon. The level mode adds more, like stars and octagons.

Is it like the "draw a perfect circle" game?

Yes, the same idea, extended to a new shape every day, with 5 tries, a global leaderboard and two more daily games.

Is it free?

Yes, free in any browser on phone or computer. No download and no account needed.
