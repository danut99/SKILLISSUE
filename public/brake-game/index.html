Brake Game: stop the car at the perfect spot

The car is coming fast. One tap to brake. Stop too early and you're miles away; too late and you crash. Skilly's Brake game is a daily timing game: a new road, speed and objective every day, the same for every player.

Play today's brake test ▶

Free · no download · works on phone and computer · new challenge every day

How to play

  1. Read today's objective: stop close to the wall, park in the bay, or stop before the red line.
  2. Check the road (dry, wet or icy) and the speed: they change your stopping distance.
  3. Tap to start, then tap again (or press Space) to brake.
  4. You have 5 guest tries per day, or 8 with a free Google-linked account. Your best stop counts.

How scoring works

Closer is better, but crossing the limit fails. Braking uses the exact moment you tap, not the screen's frame rate, so precision really is up to you.

Tips to score higher

  • On wet and icy roads the car slides much further: brake earlier.
  • Faster speed means a longer stopping distance; adjust after each try.
  • Use your first try to learn the distance, then fine-tune.

FAQ

Is the brake game free?

Yes, free in any browser on phone or computer, with no download.

Does everyone get the same road?

Yes. Road, speed and objective change every day at 00:00 UTC and are the same for everyone.

What happens if I crash?

A crash scores zero and uses a try. You can try again while you have tries left.
