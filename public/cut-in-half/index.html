Cut in Half Game: can you cut it exactly 50/50?

One shape. One straight cut. Two halves that should be exactly the same size. Skilly's Cut game gives everyone on Earth the same shape every day, and you get 5 guest tries (8 with a free account) to split it as close to 50/50 as you can. It sounds easy. It isn't.

How to play

  1. Open today's shape (a new one every day at 00:00 UTC, the same for every player).
  2. Drag one straight line across the shape and let go.
  3. The shape splits and shows both halves as percentages, for example 49.6% / 50.4%.
  4. You have 5 seconds per try and 5 guest tries per day, or 8 with a free Google-linked account. Your best cut counts.

How scoring works

Skilly measures the exact area of both pieces. 50.0 / 50.0 is a perfect 100. Every 0.1 percentage point away from 50/50 costs 0.4 points, so 49.0 / 51.0 scores 96 and 45 / 55 scores 80.

Tips to score higher

  • Aim for equal areas, not equal widths: a long thin tail holds less than it looks.
  • Find the "heavy" side of the shape first, then move your line toward it.
  • Use your earlier tries to learn: if 46 / 54 was your first cut, shift the line a little toward the bigger half.
  • Watch the halves react: the smaller piece cries, the bigger one gloats. Get it perfect for heart eyes.

FAQ

Is the cut in half game free?

Yes. Skilly is free in any browser on phone or computer, with no download and no account needed.

Does everyone get the same shape?

Yes. The shape changes every day at 00:00 UTC and is the same for every player, so you can compare scores with friends and on the global leaderboard.

What is a good score?

Anything within 1 percentage point of 50/50 (49.0 / 51.0 or better) is excellent. 50.0 / 50.0 is perfect.

Is it like Cutle or "cut the shape in half"?

Same idea: split a shape into two equal areas with one line. Skilly adds a daily shape for everyone, 5 guest tries (8 with a free account), a global leaderboard, shareable video clips and two more daily games.
